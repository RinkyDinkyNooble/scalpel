# Scalpel for NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.11

Changelog for `scalpel-neoforge-1.21.11-<version>.jar`.

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-16)

First version, for Minecraft 1.21.11 on NeoForge 21.11. It doesn't run on 1.21.9 or 1.21.10.

- From 1.21 on, enchantments are data. A redacted enchantment stays, but it does nothing and reads "Redacted". A removed one is left out of the game's data, and the tags, loot tables, advancements and other enchantments that name it are fixed up.
- Recipe viewers: JEI and Jade, because EMI has no 1.21.11 release.
