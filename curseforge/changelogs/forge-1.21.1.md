# Scalpel for Forge, Minecraft 1.21 and 1.21.1

Changelog for `scalpel-forge-1.21.1-<version>.jar`.

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-16)

First version, for Minecraft 1.21 and 1.21.1 on Forge 51 and 52.

- From 1.21 on, enchantments are data. A redacted enchantment stays, but it does nothing and reads "Redacted". A removed one is left out of the game's data, and the tags, loot tables, advancements and other enchantments that name it are fixed up.
- Recipe viewers: JEI only, because EMI and Jade have no Forge builds for this version.
